When it comes to presenting your work, I am a firmly believer that a nice looking presentation helps enormley to convey your message.
A cool presentation with catchy effects helps tremendously viewer to engange in your topic - even with very technical ones.
That was the motivation that lead me to adopt Reveal.js - a javascript framework that is design to create beautiful presentation.

What is supercool with Reveal.js is that more than having a more modern look than any beamer theme I have yet encounter (with the exception of minimalist I must admit), it allows to create dynamic presentations with cool animations.
In fact, because it relies on web technologies, virtualy any thing that could be on a website can be included in your slides.
Considering the current jungle of javascript framework, the possibility are almost infinite.

This blog post is primarly made for myself, as a memo of the different blocks I use to build my slides.
It requires basics knowledge of HTML, CSS and javascript to be understandable.
